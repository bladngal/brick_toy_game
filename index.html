Brick Garden Smash — How to Play

Everything in the garden—fountains, trees, park benches, flower beds, lamp posts, and archways—is constructed from individual interlocking toy bricks!

  • Run & Explore (WASD or Arrow Keys): Sprint along the paths and right through delicate giant flowers or piles of loose bricks to kick them around with plastic clatter.
  • Spin Kick (Left Click, F, E, or Enter): Unleash a roundhouse brick kick that cracks or explodes nearby benches, trees, fountains, and walls into tumbling 3D bricks.
  • Jump & Ground-Pound Stomp (Space): Jump on top of any garden object—or press Space again in mid-air for a super Ground-Pound—to smash it in a single hit!
  • Collect Shiny Studs: Smashed structures burst with Silver (10), Gold (100), Blue (1,000), and rare Purple (10,000) studs. Chain smashes quickly for up to a 5x Combo Multiplier!
  • Magical Brick Rebuild (R): Press R anytime (or enable Auto-Rebuild) to pull every scattered brick back through the air into its original structure.
